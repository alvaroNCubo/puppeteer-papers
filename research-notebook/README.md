# Research notebook

Working notes for the paper series: ideation for future papers, review debts, and the
per-paper collections of v0.2 changes. Everything here is working material kept public for
provenance, scrubbed for publication — no personal data, no machine internals, no
session-coordination framing.

## Layout

- **root** — active notes: open seeds and pending items.
- **`applied/`** — consumed: the note's content landed in a published paper or artifact and
  nothing in it remains pending. Kept for provenance, not for action.
- **`discarded/`** — ideas evaluated and rejected (created when the first one arrives; a
  discarded note should say *why* it was rejected).
